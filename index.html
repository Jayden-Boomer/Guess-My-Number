Hidden Number Duel

Discover your opponent's secret number using questions that contain no numbers.

Range: 1–50

Numberline Settings

How to play

  1. Each player secretly chooses a number from 1 to 50.
  2. Players alternate turns.
  3. On your turn, either ask one question or make one number guess.
  4. Questions may be open-ended, but cannot contain digits or number words.
  5. The opponent answers the question, then the turn switches.
  6. A wrong number guess also ends the turn.
  7. After a correct guess, the opponent gets one final guess to tie the game. If they miss, the first correct guess wins.
